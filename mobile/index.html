Mobile companion for HwGDReqs Desktop
Control and monitor your Geometry Dash level request queue on the fly from your Android device. This app works as a companion alongside the HwGDReqs desktop application.

Steps to Use

  • 1Requires a PC
    A PC running Geometry Dash and the HwGDReqs desktop application is required.
  • 2Install PC + Android apps
    Install HwGDReqs on your PC and this companion app (APK) on your Android device.
  • 3Follow on-screen instructions
    Open the companion app on Android and follow whatever instructions are displayed on screen to connect.
